# Security comparability of the proof diagnostics

Date: 2026-11-07 UTC. Primary-source research only; no backend changes, builds, parameter changes or proof runs. Earlier notes and frozen 033 evidence are unchanged.

**Security equivalence: NotEstablished.** The existing candidates cannot be called a comparison at the same classical security requirement. Group attack cost, a conjectured STARK soundness estimate, and an end-to-end argument-of-knowledge guarantee are different quantities. The numbers below are source-qualified indicators, not certified security levels for these composite implementations.

| Diagnostic construction | Relevant security evidence | Comparison status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: Nova standard Spartan/IPA; A1: preprocessing Spartan/IPA, Pallas/Vesta | Approximately 126-bit generic group attack estimate from Pasta's own estimator; discrete-logarithm and Fiat–Shamir/random-oracle assumptions, plus the cycle/compression construction | No end-to-end concrete bit bound established here; preprocessing does not establish a stronger security level |
| B: BN256 preprocessing HyperKZG primary / Grumpkin standard IPA secondary | Pairing-friendly BN254 requires target-field NFS analysis, not just a half-order-bit estimate. KZG also introduces SRS/trapdoor assumptions | Exact concrete bit level and equivalence to A/A1 are NotEstablished; no automatic 128-bit claim |
| C: RISC Zero v3.0.6 succinct recursive STARK, without Groth16 | Official README says 98 conjectured bits; the same pin contains other estimates and explicitly conjectural analyses | Default is not established as 128-bit security or equivalent to A/A1 |

## Nova: parameters and assumptions

Nova is pinned at `01b1e2d662a847ed7029770ecbaf6687398899dd` (0.76.0). The local [Pasta provider](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/pasta.rs#L23) identifies Pallas/Vesta and their approximately 255-bit prime orders; [engine definitions](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/mod.rs#L118) select Pedersen commitments, Poseidon random oracles and Keccak transcripts. The [constants](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/constants.rs#L3) set 128 challenge bits and 250 hash-output bits. These constants alone are not a 128-bit end-to-end theorem.

Pasta's official generator evaluates group security as `log(pi*r/12, 4)` for prime subgroup order `r`. Applying that expression to orders near `2^254` gives approximately **126 bits of generic attack work**; this is an inference from its estimator, not an exact 128-bit statement or a Nova soundness reduction. Sources: [official Pasta generator, revision `acc1384b`](https://github.com/zcash/pasta/blob/acc1384bfa7a079b7ecc59182ac821215605cd39/amicable.sage#L191), [official curve definitions](https://github.com/zcash/pasta/tree/acc1384bfa7a079b7ecc59182ac821215605cd39).

The [original Nova paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/370) and [cycle-of-curves analysis](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/969) are required provenance for the recursive construction. The [official Spartan implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/Spartan) describes its IPA variant's discrete-logarithm/random-oracle basis. That does not replace checking the precise pinned Nova compression/preprocessing composition, transcript instantiation and reduction losses. This research has not derived a concrete end-to-end bound for A or A1.

For B, the [pinned BN256/Grumpkin provider](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/bn256_grumpkin.rs#L25) fixes the actual primes. HyperKZG is a KZG-based multilinear PCS derived from Gemini; its [source introduction](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/hyperkzg.rs#L1), [unknown-tau requirement](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/hyperkzg.rs#L546) and [pairing verification](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/hyperkzg.rs#L1232) expose additional assumptions. A complete binding/knowledge-assumption and reduction audit for this exact composite remains outstanding. A successful transcript/digest check does not prove secret destruction; [the SRS provenance note](hyperkzg-srs-alternative-20261107.md) retains that distinction.

[Barbulescu–Duquesne, *Updating key size estimations for pairings*](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/334.pdf), Section 5.2, estimates roughly **100 bits** for a formerly “128-bit” BN example after SexTNFS. Its explicit parameter is `u = -2^62 - 2^55 - 1`; it must not silently be treated as an exact calculation for Nova's different Ethereum BN254 parameters. [Guillevic–Singh's later primary analysis](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/885) improves TNFS cost estimation and supplies an implementation; its exact table/result-to-pin mapping was not independently established here. Therefore neither an unqualified 100/110-bit number nor 128 bits is asserted for B. The generic group-order argument is insufficient because pairing target-field attacks must also be considered. The Grumpkin secondary proof cannot add security bits to compensate for another component.

## RISC Zero: actual default and conflicting estimates

The stable v3.0.6 source pin is `1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9`. Its [README](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/README.md#L67) advertises **98 conjectured bits**. Its [security-model table](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/website/api/security-model.md#L52) instead lists 96 for the RISC-V prover and 99 for recursion, under the random-oracle model and Toy Problem Conjecture. That table's BN254/knowledge-of-exponent/Groth16-ceremony row belongs to the optional STARK-to-SNARK wrapper; C excludes that wrapper. Succinct recursion does not erase the soundness dependencies of the execution proof it verifies.

The [pinned constants](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/risc0/zkp/src/lib.rs#L35) use 50 FRI queries, inverse expansion rate 4, folding factor 16 and minimum final degree 256. BabyBear has `p = 15*2^27+1` with [degree-4 extension](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/risc0/core/src/field/baby_bear.rs#L375). [`ProverOpts::succinct()`](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/risc0/zkvm/src/host/client/prove/opts.rs#L143) chooses Poseidon2 and the pinned allowed control IDs. [`DEFAULT_MAX_PO2`](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/risc0/zkvm/src/receipt.rs#L895) is actually **22**, despite the adjacent comment discussing a default of 21.

The [soundness calculator](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/1cc70cf05033a79ebc90f07c679cb4bd1cd301b9/risc0/zkp/src/prove/soundness.rs#L15) distinguishes Toy Problem conjectured security, other proximity/DEEP-FRI conjectures, and proven decoding bounds. Its comments give 97 conjectured bits at `2^20` cycles and 95 at `2^24`. Its Toy Model formula adds lookup, constraint and FRI errors. The isolated FRI term `(1/4)^50 = 2^-100` therefore does **not** establish 100-bit system security. No calculator was run; no single exact end-to-end number is selected from these inconsistent comments/documentation. Raising queries alone would require a newly pinned proof/verifier/control-program profile and renewed analysis, not a relabelling of C.

## Consequence for the decision

The [033 requirements](../../../specs/033-core-proof-feasibility/spec.md#L163) require explicit trust/security assumptions; [FR-017](../../../specs/033-core-proof-feasibility/spec.md#L218) requires freezing them before measurement. No explicit numerical classical 128-bit floor was found in that spec. That absence does not establish permission to exchange security strength for latency.

Before a positive security-matched performance or architecture conclusion, fix the common adversary/work model, acceptable assumptions and setup trust, numerical security target, maximum prefix/composition horizon, and exact proof/verifier parameters. Then obtain a compatible concrete analysis for every eligible candidate, including both recursive and final compression layers. A strict 128-bit floor is not established by any default profile documented here; even A's approximately 126-bit group estimate must not be presented as an exact 128-bit bound.

B may remain an explicitly conditional cost diagnostic under its frozen construction and named SRS. Its result cannot establish a positive G1 result, a security-equivalent speedup, or a production selection. Correct outputs and negative controls establish tested behavior; they do not measure cryptanalytic security. **Security equivalence remains NotEstablished.**
