# Contract: Complete Observations and Research Claims

## Existing profiles

For identical admitted module, exact parent/history, request/input/trusted context, semantic/evidence profiles and established backend contracts, require:

`Obs(Execute_experiment(x)) = Obs(Execute_reference(x))`.

Obs includes normative validation phases, ALLOW/DENY, commit refusals and performed-operation failures, selected semantic errors and order, normative traces/skips, typed observations/reads/query membership/derived values, changes, command intents and committed occurrence identity/multiplicity, canonical records, entity versions, StateId, exact history/head and applicable data/behavior/reference replay.

Compare actual canonical bytes and typed values, with first-divergence diagnostics; hashes are integrity bindings and shortcuts for locating comparisons, not independent correctness proofs. Where an observation family is inapplicable, state why rather than omit it silently. Existing 029 digests are valuable but do not alone exercise every family.

Backend call counts, implementation/debug traces and hypothetical failures of calls soundly omitted are outside equality; performed operations retain their error contracts. No necessary semantic check can disappear merely because backend call parity is not required.

## Correspondence ladder

Each candidate must identify:

1. Exact authoritative Core constructs, complete actions/effects/rules, values, typed identities, captures and evaluation order.
2. Mathematical encoding of state and simultaneous before/after delta, with identity/multiplicity and definedness conditions.
3. The theorem or written argument and every premise needed to maintain query results or complete validation observations.
4. Translation back to complete Core observations, exact parent/history/profile binding and ordinary fallback.
5. Concrete implementation/extraction trust assumptions, initialization/invalidation/reconstruction and test/mutation evidence.

Do not assume the desired invariant, successful later checks, complete query membership, absence of references or producer correctness to bypass an obligation. Partial predicates and checked arithmetic are not total mathematical group operations. Summation regrouping, early existence termination or changed branch/capture reuse needs an error/order argument. Unknown dependencies are affected.

Required adversarial coverage includes unauthorized omitted operations/policies, missing dependency edges, stale/duplicate deltas, wrong bindings/history/profile, weakened guards where applicable, insertion into an empty query, membership entry/exit, removal/reuse, aliases, overflow, partial/lazy expressions, simultaneous effects, corrupt evidence, conflicts and refused/uncertain commits. A negative control must actually detect the violated premise, not merely fail to compile unrelated code.

Selected external proof declarations may be called independently machine-checked only after pinned elaboration/kernel checking and transitive-axiom review appropriate to their own proof environment. Record theorem statements, assumptions and exact artifact identities. Source inspection and publisher claims remain different evidence classes. No Core-wide or Rust-implementation proof follows automatically.

## Prospective versioned evidence

Byte equality applies to current profiles. A future contract assessment instead defines:

`Interpret(E_new, subject, required_inputs) = Interpret(E_reference, subject, required_inputs)`.

Interpretation must include the same relevant semantics and replay/audit obligations, not just final state. State what is omitted, independently established or reconstructible, and at what cost. Bind exact module, complete applicable policy set, schema/profile, parent state and history position. Address query/range completeness, non-membership, reverse-reference completeness, fresh versus historical authority, migrations, corruption, supporting-data availability and consumer reconstruction.

An authenticated dictionary path establishes only its declared claim under its root/trust assumptions. An append-only log inclusion/consistency proof does not establish state validity or complete query results. The existing MuHash StateId is not a Merkle membership/non-membership interface. No new digest/checkpoint/index becomes governance authorization merely because it is authenticated.

Tampering, omission, stale roots, corrupt checkpoints and unavailable supporting data must not yield successful verification or authority. Unavailability, incompatibility, exhaustion and an established semantic mismatch remain distinct findings. Old evidence is never rewritten or reinterpreted to make a new format work.

## Recommendation boundary

- **Supported by evidence**: precise supported premises plus correspondence argument and necessary empirical/negative controls, with remaining trust limits explicit.
- **Rejected**: violated requirement or counterexample with retained evidence.
- **Unresolved**: concrete missing premise, measurement or verification step; no favorable implication inferred.

All five research areas receive separate dispositions. No-go conclusions are legitimate research results. They do not waive mandatory baseline measurements, expand language restrictions, modify published artifacts or authorize production delivery.
