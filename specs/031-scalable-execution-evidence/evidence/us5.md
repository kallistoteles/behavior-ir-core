# US5 — History and retention

**Historical verification: unresolved** for a safe acceleration, with **rejected** state-only or page-only shortcuts. [Costs](history-costs.md) separate 1.6-KB pages from increasing validated/reconstructed prefixes; [feasibility](history-feasibility.md) states exact anchor, corruption, cache loss, migration and authorization obligations. Within-call validated reuse is the smallest promising next step; no cache/checkpoint is granted authority here.

Three new public history tests pass, and three release-mode durable lost-ack trials preserve exact original occurrences and replay. Canonical event/occurrence order and multiplicity do not guarantee physical command scheduling or exactly-once external effects. Those remain host responsibilities.

**Bounded live-state retention: existing removal supported; general bounded capacity unresolved.** [Retention analysis](retention.md) and its actual counter control show one live entity with nine historical identity candidates and sixteen replayable events. Surviving Ref integrity, nonreuse within a lineage and historical availability remain. No implicit terminal deletion or archival protocol follows.

These are two separate dispositions. Neither a small response nor a small live set proves bounded historical/backend work. The remaining premises and needed future decisions are explicit, and no production lifecycle or persistent authority changes.
