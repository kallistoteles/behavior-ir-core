# Primary sources for the next proof architecture

Date: 2026-11-06 (UTC). Research only; no build, dependency change, setup, proof run, or timing measurement was performed. This note is outside the retained evidence. It does not revise the negative 033 assessment or make any candidate an accepted implementation.

## Contract before construction

The strict reference remains an untrusted producer and a fresh recipient that independently selects trusted verification material and checks the origin, complete program/profile, logical position, and complete endpoint. Existing FR-010–FR-016 require an actual initialization-to-compressed-certificate chain, output binding, proof production, compression, independent verification, and durable exact-parent publication before the corresponding success. Asynchronous acknowledgement cannot satisfy that existing contract. See [033 specification, lines 191–216](../spec.md#L191-L216).

Three separate choices must be recorded before comparing constructions:

| Choice | What the fresh recipient can conclude | What must be separately named |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strict certified commit | The accepted proof establishes the declared relation from its trusted origin through the entire certified prefix, ending at the expected endpoint; publication additionally establishes the declared durable canonical head. | Proof/certificate representation, trusted release and origin, output tuple, publication authority and durability assumptions. |
| Immediate local K plus asynchronous prefix proof | The local participant has checked a transition against its authenticated parent. Until the new prefix proof completes, a fresh recipient has the previously certified prefix plus an uncertified suffix. | Separate local acknowledgement and certified-finality states, certified position versus accepted position, backlog/recovery rules. Local K does not prove that an otherwise untrusted parent has a valid entire prefix. |
| Signed trusted checkpoint plus local K | The recipient trusts the checkpoint issuer for all history summarized by that checkpoint and checks the declared suffix relation. | Issuer authority, key lifecycle, equivocation/revocation rules, exact checkpoint position. This deliberately adds trust; hashing or signing a snapshot does not prove its reachable origin. |

The latter two rows are contract analysis, not claims about a library. A proof of a valid transition also does not establish that its branch is canonical, that commands executed externally, or that stored evidence remains available. Preserve those distinctions when fixing guarantees. State/output validity, origin validity, history binding, canonical publication, durable acknowledgement, and evidence reconstruction each need a named mechanism. Existing [FR-012–FR-015](../spec.md#L200-L212) already separates several of these obligations.

Recursive verification and fresh recompression are distinct operations. Nova exposes `RecursiveSNARK::verify` separately from `CompressedSNARK::{setup,prove,verify}`; compression randomizes/folds instances and produces primary and secondary SNARKs. A retained recursive artifact can therefore be a separately measured certificate representation, but skipping `CompressedSNARK::prove` changes the 033 certificate profile. A previously compressed endpoint also cannot be reused as a certificate of a newly extended endpoint. Sources: pinned [recursive verification, lines 570–680](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L570-L680), [compression setup/prove, lines 768–891](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L768-L891), and [FR-014](../spec.md#L206-L208).

## A. Keep the standard Nova path as the controlled comparison

The upstream compression test instantiates Pallas/Vesta with IPA; the engine definitions select Pedersen commitments. Standard Spartan's verifier key stores `R1CSShape`, and its setup pads/stores that shape. Thus short proof bytes do not establish a small cold verifier bundle or verification independent of the step relation. Sources: [pinned compression instantiations, lines 1381–1392](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L1381-L1392), [Pallas/Vesta engines, lines 118–147](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/mod.rs#L118-L147), [standard verifier key/setup, lines 38–109](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/spartan/snark.rs#L38-L109). The original [Nova paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/370) describes the transparent folding construction and its additional succinct proof of knowledge; this does not imply that the later HyperKZG variant is ceremony-free.

Use A to hold the certificate contract and existing semantic encoding constant. Its next useful discriminator is an independently specified identity relation with the complete publication wrapper charged separately. If its cryptographic lower bound exceeds the agreed gate, stop before authenticating Core state. Passing that lower bound only permits a real authenticated-transition trial; it is not evidence of Core semantics or the final circuit's cost.

Preprocessing and HyperKZG are independent axes. The same pinned source also instantiates Pallas/Vesta+IPA with preprocessing on both sides; if the no-ceremony condition is fixed, that is a controlled variant of A worth screening before declaring ceremony acceptance necessary. It must have a different construction identity, and preprocessing time/retained memory remain charged. Source: [preprocessing test and instantiations, lines 1395–1470](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L1395-L1470).

## B. HyperKZG is a real route with a different setup and field

The pinned benchmark gives exact types: `Bn256EngineKZG`, `GrumpkinEngine`, primary `hyperkzg::EvaluationEngine`, secondary `ipa_pc::EvaluationEngine`. Its `SS1` is preprocessing Spartan and `SS2` is standard Spartan. The separate upstream test instantiates preprocessing on both sides. These are distinct configurations, so the phrase “Nova preprocessing HyperKZG” is insufficient to identify a run. Sources: [benchmark types, lines 11–20](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/benches/compressed-snark.rs#L11-L20), [both-preprocessing instantiation, lines 1460–1468](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L1460-L1468). The official [Nova README at the pin](https://github.com/microsoft/Nova/blob/01b1e2d662a847ed7029770ecbaf6687398899dd/README.md#L10-L21) distinguishes ordinary and preprocessing compression and pairing-based commitments.

Crucial setup boundary: the benchmark calls ordinary `PublicParams::setup`, but the pinned HyperKZG implementation enables random-tau setup only under tests or `test-utils`. Its production implementation returns a setup error and directs callers to ceremony files. Generating one's own known tau is explicitly documented as insecure; random entropy does not turn that test facility into an acceptable ceremony. Sources: [test-only key generation, lines 344–357](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/hyperkzg.rs#L344-L357), [production rejection, lines 550–577](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/hyperkzg.rs#L550-L577), [benchmark setup call, lines 58–64](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/benches/compressed-snark.rs#L58-L64).

The production API is `PublicParams::setup_with_ptau_dir`, gated by `io`, loading the primary commitment key from an appropriately sized pruned ptau file and using normal secondary key generation. The inspected 033 dependency has `default-features = false`, so substituting types alone cannot enable that path. Sources: [API and feature gate, lines 187–248](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/nova/mod.rs#L187-L248), [dependency declaration](../../../experiments/transition-study033/Cargo.toml#L18), [vendor features](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/Cargo.toml#L91-L98). A future isolated trial must pin ceremony/transcript origin, verified input digests, conversion/pruning tool and output digests, SRS capacity, and trusted installation path. No ceremony artifact has been acquired or validated in this research.

BN256/Grumpkin changes primary/secondary scalar fields, commitments and parameter/transcript identities relative to Pallas/Vesta. Re-establish range/byte/hash and arithmetic correspondence over those actual fields; do not reuse an old field-specific theorem solely because both implement `Engine`. Sources: [BN256/Grumpkin engines, lines 50–83](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/mod.rs#L50-L83) and [Pallas/Vesta, lines 126–147](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova/src/provider/mod.rs#L126-L147). The source establishes availability, not that preprocessing or pairings reduce this project's full latency. Whole-cost and memory differences remain unknown.

## C. A concrete transparent alternative: RISC Zero succinct STARKs

RISC Zero documents a succinct receipt as one recursive STARK for the session and supports composition of succinct receipts. Groth16 is a subsequent optional wrapper, with a separate ceremony. The selected candidate stops at the succinct STARK. Sources: official [proof-system overview](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/website/docs/proof-system/proof-system.md) and [Groth16 setup description](https://dev.risczero.com/api/trusted-setup-ceremony). “No ceremony” does not remove trust in the chosen verifier implementation, control-root parameters, guest image or cryptographic assumptions.

The implementation is concrete: [Prover API](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/host/client/prove/mod.rs) supports local proving via `prove`, an explicit succinct option, and compression via recursive lift/join/resolve; [server compression](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/host/server/prove/mod.rs#L178-L254) implements receipt-kind routing; [recursion implementation](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/host/recursion/prove/mod.rs) implements the composition operations. Explicitly choose local proving and record the selected backend so ambient environment settings cannot silently move measurement to a service.

A Core guest can check a bounded authenticated transition and write the complete semantic subject/output to its journal. To establish a whole prefix without replay, it must verify the predecessor claim with the expected guest image, predecessor journal and exact parent binding, or implement an explicitly proved initialization case. Guest [`env::verify`](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/guest/env/verify.rs) records a composition assumption; resolving it is part of final proof production. Merely executing the guest or producing a conditional claim leaves work outstanding. This paragraph is a proposed application design, not an existing Core integration.

Acceptance must pin the trusted image ID, verifier parameters and allowed control root, require the succinct receipt kind, disable development mode, and compare every expected journal field. `Receipt::verify_with_context` checks the expected successful image/claim and journal binding; integrity-only verification has weaker application checks. Receipt metadata is explicitly unauthenticated. Sources: [receipt API](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/receipt.rs), [succinct receipt parameters](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/receipt/succinct.rs), and [options/dev-mode controls](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkvm/src/host/client/prove/opts.rs). A valid receipt for an unrelated guest or wrong but internally consistent output is insufficient.

The inspected source provides a [CPU HAL](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/zkp/src/hal/cpu.rs) and [CPU recursion implementation](https://github.com/risc0/risc0/blob/164c64bb3a844d393bb7addc5781c705c647f2d5/risc0/circuit/recursion/src/prove/hal/cpu.rs). This establishes a CPU source path, not binary compatibility with the Xeon X5670 or a practical memory bound. Complete dependency/ISA audit and an actual portable identity receipt on that host are mandatory discriminators. No ADX, BMI2, AVX2, SHA-extension, GPU, memory or toolchain support is assumed. No Rust/native Core correspondence is inherited automatically from proving an ELF.

Plonky2 also has concrete cyclic recursion, but its official README declares deprecation/no further updates, requires nightly, and notes default security caveats. It is excluded from the chosen third candidate rather than silently becoming the maintained alternative. Sources: [official README](https://github.com/0xPolygonZero/plonky2/blob/5d9da5a65bbcba2c66eb29c035090eb2e9ccb05f/README.md) and [cyclic-recursion implementation](https://github.com/0xPolygonZero/plonky2/blob/5d9da5a65bbcba2c66eb29c035090eb2e9ccb05f/plonky2/src/recursion/cyclic_recursion.rs). No broader survey was performed.

## Minimal discriminators before an architecture decision

All items below are proposed and **NotRun**. Use new isolated artifacts and identities; preserve frozen 033 sources/evidence. Do not rerun old Nova cases to fill an apparent comparison table, and do not start builds/proving while the current resource headroom is insufficient.

1. **Contract and identity freeze.** Record strict prefix/finality as the baseline and label any relaxed alternative separately. Freeze source/lock/toolchain/overlays, CPU target/features, construction on each side, security/hash/encoding profiles, verifier trust roots, exact relation/shape or guest image, proof form, setup provenance, logical-event versus machine-step count, and success endpoints. Give preparation and resident/cold paths separate budgets before executing them.
2. **Identity lower bound.** One fixed identity machine with one independently expected output tuple; measure setup/key loading, initialize/extend, required compression or STARK recursion, serialize, cold independent verify and endpoint comparison. Test wrong expected output, altered origin/position/profile, malformed proof, wrong verification material, and fake/development receipt where applicable. Unchanged-root identity tests cannot pass the semantic trial. Gate and stop rule must precede timing; an identity pass only rules out that measured lower-bound failure.
3. **Actual tiny authenticated transition.** Keep the same cap-2 Core fixture across candidates: independently fixed genesis/root and request, one checked integer update/local predicate, authenticated old leaf/path and new root, exact-parent binding, complete ordered outputs/commands and final phase. Use independently calculated expected bytes/roots/output fixtures, not a second invocation of the prover's own output generator. Reject changed sibling/key/value/root, wrong request/context/parent, overflow, wrong count result, wrong output/ordering, invalid start and unfinished phase through the actual consumer path. Keep correct semantic refusals distinct from proof failure or operational failure. Original 1000/1001 boundary cases remain a later mandatory test, not a success inferred from cap 2.
4. **Prefix-specific controls.** Two real transitions with a fresh recipient receiving only trusted verifier/origin material and the endpoint certificate/support declared by the profile. Reject substitution of a valid predecessor from another origin or branch, wrong logical position, skipped transition, stale certified endpoint and unclosed composition assumption. Local K alone cannot discharge these prefix tests. Separately establish actual circuit/ELF/native correspondence; finite controls are necessary but do not replace FR-011's mathematical evidence.
5. **Charge the full acknowledgement path.** Record wall time and peak resources from submitted request through authenticated reads/witness preparation, native K, all proof microsteps or zkVM execution, compression/lift/join/resolve, serialization, independent endpoint verification, exact-parent CAS/transaction, durable publication of state/history/evidence/result/commands, and acknowledgement. Separately record cold setup/install/SRS validation, genesis/import, restart/first useful operation, proof-continuation loading or recovery, fresh consumer and reconstruction. Include bytes and retained disk/RAM for parameters, prover/verifier keys, continuation state and final certificate; include losing branches/retries and distinguish uncertain acknowledgement recovery. Overlap only when measured with a declared dependency graph; do not sum overlapped phases or present compute-only time as committed latency.

The hardware reference is material: retained 033 evidence reports Xeon X5670 without ADX/BMI2 and earlier SIGILL before cryptographic measurement; the portable patch selects another existing arithmetic path. See [retained scoped-chain log, line 1809](../evidence/scoped-chain/README.md#L1809) and [patch](../../../experiments/transition-study033/vendor/nova033-portable.patch). The success of that selected Nova path cannot establish compatibility for a new curve, pairing implementation, native dependency, prebuilt zkVM tool, or remote GPU result. Report actual ISA/resource failures as such, not as semantic rejection or negative control success.

The decision after these discriminators is intentionally open: available source does not establish that any candidate meets the fixed guarantees and total latency budget. Preserve an explicit negative outcome if identity or the smallest genuine authenticated transition already fails its gate.
