## Predeclared research discriminators

These are proposed finite diagnostic gates, not statistically established SLA thresholds. Raw observations/ranges remain visible even when a criterion fails.

- **Correctness prerequisite:** zero unexplained complete native semantic mismatches, all required applicable controls, original Safety obligations, fixed theorem/type/axiom/fresh-check inventory and actual treatment activation. Any failure blocks positive dependent comparisons.
- **Ground mechanism:** a retained exact ground must eliminate the selected redundant full re-establishment pass; at N=256,H=8 report its operation and phase differences against matched G0. A material ground-phase benefit requires at least 2× lower median re-establishment time where the reference phase is nonzero. This alone does not establish local complete execution.
- **Semantic locality:** GV finish and its eligible drain must visit zero old-prefix events and must not traverse all unrelated live objects/maps to derive their semantic result/view delta. The general dependency/work argument must hold for the declared fragment, with actual logarithmic/index overhead disclosed. These numeric timing gates use only the three timing-mode phase observations; detailed-counter timings never substitute. A full record encoder is excluded from this local claim but included in complete work.
- **Strong local feasibility:** at N=256,H=8, combined native decision plus mandatory affected-view semantic work has a diagnostic median ≤50 ms and at most one quarter of the matched R semantic phase. Necessary validity work cannot be moved outside this phase solely to satisfy the target. Mathematical/locality conclusions remain separate if this timing gate fails.
- **Complete endpoint:** call a complete-operation benefit material only if its median is ≤80% of matched R at N=256,H=8 under the same original-output guarantees. Also record a directly timed primary-plus-drain cycle. A recommendation to integrate a candidate requires neither primary complete-operation nor primary-plus-drain cycle median >110% of matched R in any completed cell, plus all applicable quality/correctness gates. Do not add separate medians to synthesize the cycle. The extra drain is not one of the 60 primary timing observations. Without an end-to-end win, report the valid component/locality result and residual observation/identity floor instead of claiming full performance feasibility.
- **No statistical inflation:** three timing observations support raw/range/diagnostic median only. No p50/p95, capacity or significance claim. Do not sum independent medians to manufacture a complete endpoint.

A missing or confounded comparison makes its discriminator unestablished. Completing the investigation never converts a failed positive criterion into success.
