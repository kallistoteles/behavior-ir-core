# Ground retention — development assessment

Actual R/G processes now retain or discard the selected engine-owned snapshot
certificate on the **same `Store<Capture>` backend**. Each process first checks
the original Safety program. Acquisition reexecutes the original prefix, checks
the original initial and resulting Safety properties, compares the entire
captured creation/commit packet and independently expected endpoint, and keeps
the actual checked sample Store. Imported reports cannot construct `Ground` or
`CheckedProgram`; neither capability implements deserialization or cloning.

The selected Core certificate remains bound by Core's existing exact
`SnapshotIdentity`: full admitted module content, schema, validation profile and
complete history reference. The study's owned ground additionally binds the
original Safety analysis and exclusive process-local writer boundary. G0 drops
selected engine evidence before invoking; G1 retains evidence already obtained
by actual checks. Both still run ordinary invocation, original commit
rederivation, full original post-Safety validation and complete observation
materialization. Original encoders and identities are unchanged.

The retained R/G executables in
[`ground-workers-001`](evidence/development/ground-workers-001/workers.json)
are different actual binaries with separate successful process receipts.
Mechanism-sensitive observations show **one full snapshot-validation pass for
R, zero for G**, on both D1's primary finish and its unchanged-content drain.
Both compare the entire original result to the unpatched oracle before writing
and syncing the required observation. These are single development work-mode
observations; their times are not final timing samples or latency distributions.

The lifecycle tests check wrong expected history, changed policy/writer,
invalid origin, reset/retain, full original equality, confirmed continuation,
stale expectations, failure/conflict and lost acknowledgement. The fault backend
is explicitly simulated: in the lost-ack case it actually applies an in-memory
commit and captures its arguments before returning an error. Ground authority
is quarantined, and revival requires original reexecution/comparison at the
independently expected actual child. A restart transfers only unverified data.
This establishes no physical durability or Q6 result.

What survives is checked exact-snapshot knowledge and the common verified
prefix provenance. Unsupported ownership/context changes, mutable backend
access, wrong expected history or uncertainty cannot manufacture a child
certificate. Parent/child identity includes the event: equal contents at a new
position do not mean the old ground applies.

Most cost sources remain: ordinary population evaluation, complete version
materialization and hashing in `versions_for`, copying/hashing current and
previous original history records, original Safety checking, mandatory original
record construction, commit and output. Acquisition still pays for historical
reexecution. No V/GV execution, local native-work bound, scaling benefit or
production integration is established by this increment.

The finite typed finish/preservation/ground laws have twelve type/axiom/fresh
kernel-checked roots. Native Rust projection, encoder/observer fidelity,
publication and host correspondence remain separately named premises/gaps.
Native agreement and a real removed validation pass do not prove a complete
Rust-to-Lean refinement. The next phase must implement and test actual prepared
invocation and maintained views before freezing a performance campaign.
